BITS WILP Progress

BITS WILP Progress helps Semester 1 learners revise course concepts through structured quizzes and track completed questions, daily milestones and study streaks.

The public revision content can be viewed without signing in. Progress is saved locally in the browser by default.

What the app does

This application is an educational revision tool covering Advanced Structural Mechanics & Stability, Dynamics of Structures, Finite Element Analysis and Advanced Steel Structures. It provides incremental quiz questions, formula practice and progress tracking to support independent study.

Google sign-in is optional. When an eligible @wilp.bits-pilani.ac.in user chooses to sign in from a course page, Google account information is used to authenticate the user and associate study progress with that account so progress can be synchronized across devices. The application does not request a Google password.

For details about how account and progress information is handled, read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Independent project: BITS WILP Progress is a study aid and is not an official BITS Pilani academic system.
