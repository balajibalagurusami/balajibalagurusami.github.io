Formula reminder

Kφ = ω²Mφ; modal mass Mₙ = φₙᵀMφₙ; u = Φq.

q(t) = q(0) cos(ωt) + q̇(0) sin(ωt)/ω for undamped free vibration.

C = a₀M + a₁K; ζₙ = a₀/(2ωₙ) + a₁ωₙ/2; ωd = ωₙ√(1−ζ²).
