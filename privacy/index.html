Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the revision website handles information when you use the quizzes, progress tracking and optional BITS cloud sign-in.

1. Information stored on your device

The site stores revision progress in your browser using localStorage. This can include completed question IDs, the last question you opened, daily attempt counts, milestones and streak information.

The site may also use small first-party cookies to remember limited progress or session-related information. Browser storage remains on your device unless you use the optional cloud sync feature.

2. Optional BITS cloud sign-in

If you sign in with an eligible @wilp.bits-pilani.ac.in Google account, the site receives the account information required to authenticate you, including your email address. The site then syncs supported revision-progress data between your devices.

Google authentication is handled using Google Identity Services. Your Google password is not collected by this website.

3. Cloud progress data

When cloud sync is enabled, supported progress data may be sent to the site backend and stored so that it can be merged with progress from another device. The synced data is intended for study progress and streak continuity.

4. Information we do not intentionally collect

  • We do not ask for payment-card details.
  • We do not ask for your Google password.
  • We do not sell personal information to advertisers.
  • We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the quiz interface.

5. Third-party services

The site may rely on third-party infrastructure or services such as GitHub Pages, Cloudflare and Google Identity Services. Those providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and operational requirements.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can clear locally stored progress using your browser's site-data controls. If you use cloud sync, signing out stops further authenticated syncing from that browser. Cloud-stored progress may remain until it is deleted from the service backend.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the service, but no website or online storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Educational purpose

This website is a study and revision aid. It is not an official BITS Pilani academic system unless expressly stated otherwise.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website, authentication flow or progress-sync features change. The effective date shown above will be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to this site, use the contact channel provided by the site administrator.
