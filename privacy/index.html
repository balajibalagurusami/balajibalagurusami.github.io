BITS WILP Progress

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BITS WILP Progress handles information when you use its public revision content, quizzes, progress tracking and optional Google sign-in/cloud-sync features.

1. Public use without sign-in

The study and revision content can be viewed without signing in. By default, supported revision progress is stored locally in your browser.

2. Information stored on your device

The site uses browser localStorage for revision progress. This can include completed question IDs, the last question opened, daily attempt counts, milestones and streak information.

The site may also use small first-party cookies for limited progress or authenticated-session functionality.

3. Optional Google sign-in and Google user data

If you choose to sign in with a Google account, Google Identity Services may provide basic account information such as your name, email address and profile picture. Google does not provide your Google password to BITS WILP Progress.

BITS WILP Progress uses this Google account information for authentication and to associate synchronized study progress with the signed-in account. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Cloud progress data

When you use optional cloud sync, supported quiz and study-progress information may be sent to the application backend and stored so progress can be merged across devices. This may include completed-question state, last-opened question information, daily attempt counts, milestones and streak information.

5. How information is used

  • To provide the revision and progress-tracking features.
  • To authenticate users who voluntarily choose Google sign-in.
  • To synchronize supported study progress between devices.
  • To maintain session and service security.

6. Information we do not intentionally collect

  • We do not ask for payment-card details.
  • We do not ask for or store your Google password.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the quiz interface.

7. Third-party services

The service may rely on infrastructure or services including GitHub Pages, Cloudflare and Google Identity Services. Those providers may process technical or account information according to their own policies and operational requirements.

8. Data retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored progress through your browser's site-data controls. Signing out stops further authenticated synchronization from that browser. Cloud-synced progress may remain on the service backend until it is deleted or the service retention process removes it. Requests relating to cloud-stored account or progress data can be directed to the site administrator through the contact channel provided for the service.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the service and authenticated sessions, but no website or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Educational purpose and independence

BITS WILP Progress is an independent study and revision aid. It is not an official BITS Pilani academic, examination or grading system.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website, authentication flow or progress-sync features change. The effective date will be revised when material changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests relating to BITS WILP Progress, use the contact channel provided by the site administrator.
