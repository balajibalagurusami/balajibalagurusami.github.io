Respected Sir/Madam,

I respectfully submit this representation seeking implementation of the final order dated 01.09.2026 passed by the Hon'ble High Court of Judicature at Madras in W.P. No. 28112 of 2026, and requesting that necessary action may kindly be taken for my appointment to the post of Assistant Section Officer (ASO) under the Combined Civil Services Examination – Group V-A Services, pursuant to TNPSC Notification No. 16 of 2025 dated 07.10.2025.

I participated in the selection process for the post of Assistant Section Officer under the above notification with Registration No. 0101003078. I had secured 151.75 marks and was initially shortlisted for On-Screen Certificate Verification. Subsequently, my candidature was not included for Physical Certificate Verification on the ground that I was holding the designation of Accountant in the Department of Treasuries and Accounts instead of the designation of Assistant.

Aggrieved by the same, I approached the Hon'ble High Court by filing W.P. No. 28112 of 2026, seeking a direction to consider my claim for appointment to the post of Assistant Section Officer and to appoint me with all consequential service benefits.

During the pendency of the writ petition, the Hon'ble High Court, by its interim order dated 20.07.2026, directed that I be permitted to participate in the Physical Certificate Verification, if otherwise eligible, and further directed that one post be kept vacant till the disposal of the writ petition.

After hearing all the parties and considering the relevant Government Orders and Service Rules, the Hon'ble High Court has specifically held that the designation of Assistant in the Department of Treasuries and Accounts was merely redesignated as Accountant under G.O.Ms.No.757, Finance (Treasuries and Accounts) Department, dated 29.05.1979, and that such redesignation did not alter the nature of the post, scale of pay or service conditions.

The Hon'ble Court further held that an Accountant in the Department of Treasuries and Accounts continues to discharge the same functions as an Assistant in other departments and that denial of consideration merely on the ground that I hold the designation of Accountant instead of Assistant was not sustainable. The Hon'ble Court accordingly answered the issues in my favour.

The Hon'ble Court has also recorded that I possess the requisite service qualification, having completed 3 years and 3 months as Junior Assistant and 3 years, 1 month and 11 days as Accountant, totalling 6 years, 4 months and 18 days of qualifying experience.

The Hon'ble Court further observed that I had secured marks above the prescribed cut-off and was therefore entitled to be considered for the post. With regard to the contention that all vacancies had already been filled, the Hon'ble Court observed that such contention “does not inspire confidence”, particularly in view of the interim direction dated 20.07.2026 requiring one post to be kept vacant.

Finally, the Hon'ble High Court has been pleased to order as follows:

“Accordingly, the writ petition is allowed as prayed for.”

The prayer allowed by the Hon'ble Court was for consideration of my claim for the post of Assistant Section Officer and for my appointment to the said post with all consequential service benefits.

In view of the above final order of the Hon'ble High Court, I therefore most respectfully request the Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission, the Human Resource Management Department and the Directorate of Treasuries and Accounts to kindly take immediate necessary action to implement the order dated 01.09.2026 in W.P. No. 28112 of 2026, arrange for my appointment to the post of Assistant Section Officer under Group V-A Services and extend all consequential service benefits in accordance with the Hon'ble Court's order.

I am enclosing a copy of the final order dated 01.09.2026 for your kind reference and necessary action.

I request that the matter may kindly be processed and appropriate appointment orders issued at the earliest.
